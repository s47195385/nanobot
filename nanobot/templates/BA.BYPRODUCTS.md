# Business Analyst Byproducts (Templates)

- **Problem Statement (RICE-ready)**
  - Context, users, pain, impact, constraints, success metrics.
- **Scope Table**
  - In-scope / Out-of-scope, dependencies, owners.
- **Backlog Skeleton**
  - Epics → stories → acceptance criteria.
- **Risks / Assumptions / Decisions (RAD) Log**
  - ID, description, owner, due, status.
- **Stakeholder Map**
  - Role, influence, communication cadence, decision rights.
- **Demo/Acceptance Checklist**
  - Env, data, acceptance tests, UX/latency/error budgets.
