# Business Analyst Workflows

## Requirements Discovery (0 → 1)
- Map stakeholders, roles, and decision owners.
- Capture goals, constraints, timelines, success metrics, and budget.
- Identify scope boundaries (in / out) and dependencies.
- Interview script (minimal):
  - Who is the user? What is the core job-to-be-done?
  - What is painful today? What is the happy-path definition of done?
  - What data, systems, and teams are involved?
  - What are must-haves vs. nice-to-haves?
- Produce: problem statement, goals, risks, acceptance criteria draft.

## Existing System Assessment
- Inventory current assets: repos, services, interfaces, SLAs, data contracts.
- Trace upstream/downstream impacts and compliance concerns.
- Capture gaps: reliability, scalability, observability, UX, security.
- Produce: gap/impact matrix and remediation options with effort/benefit.

## Translate to Technical Requirements
- Derive capabilities → features → user stories.
- Define data inputs/outputs, non-functionals, and success metrics.
- Align stories with acceptance tests and demo criteria.
- Produce: concise PRD + backlog (epics, stories, sequencing).

## Agile Planning Loop
- Groom backlog (slice thin, de-risk unknowns with spikes).
- Plan capacity, dependencies, and milestones.
- Establish cadences: standup, review, retro, demo.
- Produce: sprint plan, risks/assumptions/decisions log, comms plan.
