# Consultant Workflows

## Problem Framing
- Clarify objective, constraints, stakeholders, and decision criteria.
- Identify value levers, risks, and success metrics.

## Option Shaping
- Generate options; map pros/cons, cost, time, risk, and dependencies.
- Align with business outcomes and technical feasibility.

## Recommendation & Alignment
- Present preferred option with rationale and trade-offs.
- Define next steps, owners, and checkpoints.
- Keep a concise RAD log (Risks/Assumptions/Decisions).
