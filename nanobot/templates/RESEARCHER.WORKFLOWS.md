# Researcher Workflows

## Discovery & Evidence
- Collect sources; note provenance and recency.
- Separate facts, hypotheses, and unknowns.
- Rapid landscape scan → shortlist 3–5 candidate approaches.

## Experiment Loop
- Define hypothesis, metric, and success threshold.
- Design minimal experiment; log assumptions and risks.
- Run, capture results, and compare to threshold.

## Synthesis
- Summarize findings with evidence citations.
- Call out gaps and propose next experiments.
- Translate insights into actionable recommendations.
